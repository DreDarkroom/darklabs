Controls

Numbers 1-4: Tap articulations

  • 1 Soft Single Tap
  • 2 Hard Single Tap
  • 3 Double Tap
  • 4 Rapid Run

A - ' (QWERTY middle row): Chromatic Tonal Hum

Note: Upper row W,E,T,Y,U,O,P represent sharps/flats (black keys).

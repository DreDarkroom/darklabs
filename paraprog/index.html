ParaProg

Drum theory you can hear. Paradiddles, time signatures, how to count the weird ones, and what Tool and friends are really doing.

Every sound is synthesised in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Tap Play to start the audio.

Song time signatures are flagged checked or approximate. Where sources disagree, the app says so rather than guessing. Spot a mistake? Open an issue on the Darklabs repository.

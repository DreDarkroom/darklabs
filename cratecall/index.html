    Loop & export

    Turn on Trim under a music track to drag its loop start/end points on the waveform, then Loop to preview it. Export gives you the trimmed region as a lossless WAV (for further editing) or a 320kbps MP3 (for a quick share) — both are built right in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

    About

    CrateCall is DreDarkroom's audition tool for the VRUS game's audio pool — music and SFX candidates. Verdicts and notes are saved to your own browser; use Send selections to email your picks in. Tracks found since your last visit here are flagged NEW.

    CC0 sources worth checking

    • OpenGameArt.orgreliableFilter by licence — CC0 on the search page. Every track in this pool was checked on its own item page, not just the folder name.
    • Freesound.orgreliableLicence is set per sound, not per pack — check each file's own page.
    • Kenney.nlreliableKenney releases everything CC0 by policy — a safe default for game SFX.
    • itch.ioMixed licensing site-wide. Some asset packs are CC0, most aren't — always check the page, never assume from "free".
    • PixabayNot CC0 since Jan 2019 — it's the Pixabay Content License now, which has its own terms. This is the mix-up that caught Hackerman's gun sounds; treat anything from here as licensed, not CC0.

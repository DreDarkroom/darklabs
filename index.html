MeowSynth

A tiny browser synth made entirely of human "meow" impressions. Play chromatic melodies and trigger mood pads with a single click.

Open MeowSynth

Testing APK for Android:

  1. Download the .apk file from the releases
  2. Enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in your Android settings
  3. Tap the downloaded file to install
  4. Open MeowSynth app and enjoy

ThroatTapper

Percussion made from throat flicks and taps. Four tap articulations on keys 1–4, plus a chromatic tonal hum across the QWERTY middle row.

Press DROP MODE to switch it on first. Styling pass still to come.

CircuitStomp

A drum & bass machine built entirely from synthesised robot voices — no samples anywhere. Sequencer with pitch and chance per step, live playing, MIDI in and out, and exports of stems, MIDI and a ready-made Reaper project. Includes 11 lessons and a printable guide.

Open CircuitStomp

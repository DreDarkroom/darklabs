CircuitStomp

A drum & bass machine built entirely from synthesised robot voices — no samples anywhere. Sequencer with pitch and chance per step, live playing, MIDI in and out, and exports of stems, MIDI and a ready-made Reaper project. Includes 11 lessons and a printable guide.

Open CircuitStomp

Darklabs Radio

A cleared-for-use radio station with a TTS presenter reading idents between tracks — built to be hosted or broadcast, with room to adapt to other environments later on.

Presenter voice is placeholder browser TTS until real voice pops exist. No shared live stream yet — each listener's browser plays its own loop.

Open Darklabs Radio

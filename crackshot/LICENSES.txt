CRACKSHOT: LICENCES AND CREDITS
===============================

Every sound in this pack is derived from real recordings that other people have shared under open licences,
plus (a) a plain sine-wave "thump" computed by code and (b) a very quiet layer from a Kenney CC0 pack.
No AI audio model was used. All processing is classic DSP (filters, saturation, envelope shaping).

WHAT WAS DONE TO THEM
---------------------
Sliced into single shots, DC and rumble removed, tail shortened, transient shaped, brightened (exciter + EQ),
saturated (crunch), a short synthesised sine "thump" added for weight, soft-limited and normalised.
These are modified versions. The processed files in this pack are released under CC0 1.0 where all inputs were CC0.
Files derived from the CC BY 3.0 source (if any are ever added) must carry the attribution above.

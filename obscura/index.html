DreDarkroom

A beat-driven DJ who learned to see under a crimson safelight. The darkroom taught patience, timing and a feel for light, and those crafts came with me into the digital world. Two sides, one room: Safelight for calm and melody, SquidgySqueegee for bounce and swing.

Before anything was digital there was a darkroom: a crimson safelight, the smell of chemistry and a blank sheet slowly finding its picture.

02

Stop · timing

Pull a print too early and it's thin; leave it too long and it's mud. A blend works the same way.

I'm a beat-driven DJ, and every set I play leans on that darkroom clock.

03

Fix · what stuck

Fixer makes an image permanent. What the darkroom fixed in me was patience, framing and a feel for light.

It all came with me into the digital world: stills, video, timelapse and now sets sequenced like a contact sheet.

04 · The lab arcade

Play in the dark

Two little games made from darkroom parts. Every win exposes a little more of the roll.

Silver Rush

Hold the pads

Your film canister harvests silver. Spend it on crew, hold two of the three pads to charge the flash, and fire it at the light leak. Three flashes and their print is fixed.

Silver

Base plates
3 · 3

Pick a crew card (1 · 2 · 3), then tap a lane on the board (or ← ↑ →). Squeegees beat Glares, Tongs beat Beams, Enlargers beat Flares. They counter you too.

Light Dash

Catch the light

Steer with the mouse, a finger or WASD. Click, tap or Space to dash. Dashing through a light leak develops it; touching one without dashing fogs you. Hold the ring for a bonus.

BlueHeronBass_

a five-string bass with a heron in its throat — pluck it, slap it, fuzz it, loop it

(browsers block audio until you interact once — one tap and it's live)

Works in a Quest / VR browser as a flat page: point and click the strings.

Keys oct 0

touch a string to play it · slide along it to glide between frets · several fingers = several strings · computer keys: the Z and Q rows play like a piano from low E (Esc = panic)

Amp — compressor, blended fuzz (lows stay clean), envelope filter, EQ

Heron — synthesised calls; they also grow out of every note and can excite the strings

Loop & groove

Record & export

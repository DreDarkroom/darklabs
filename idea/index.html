Drunk Bots

Two teams of identical bots have wandered into a very large flat-pack maze and are quite, quite sure they know the way out.

Pick a team. Shout directions. First team to get 4 bots to the checkout wins.

VR is a simple tabletop view: the whole store sits on a table in front of you, and you point a controller at a bot and squeeze to shout.

A parody. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or related to any real furniture retailer, living or flat-packed. All shelves, hex keys and bots are made up.

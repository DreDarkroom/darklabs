PENROSE

an infinite staircase of sound

tap to resume
Intro D minor · lap 1

 

C3–B5

How to play (start here)
  1. Start soft. Play a few slow notes. This is a felt piano, close and quiet, and nothing else is happening yet.
  2. Press Climb. The arc starts by itself: Intro (just piano), Climb (a pad, then a circling figure, then bass, hats and kick, with the filter opening), Build (a riser and a snare roll, then the kick drops out), Peak (the drop, everything open), Release (it all falls away back to the piano).
  3. It never ends. After the release the key lifts a whole step and the arc starts again, one step higher. Six laps and you are back where you began. The ripple map under the slider shows where you are.
  4. Play over it. The glowing keys are the notes that fit the chord right now, and the piano figure steps aside while you play. A short phrase of your own (2 to 5 notes) becomes the figure the piano and the arpeggio circle.
  5. Or steer. Touch the Ascent slider and the arc holds wherever you put it, so you can sit in the Intro for as long as you like, or stay at the edge of the drop. Press Climb to let it carry on from there.
  6. Pads turn the keyboard into eight pads that only play notes from the current chord, so you can tap rhythms like on a kit and nothing can sound wrong.
Tone & harmony

Touch, mouse, your keyboard (A–', Z/X octave, Space sustain) or a MIDI keyboard (mod wheel = Ascent). Slide across keys for glissando; press lower on a key to play louder.

An infinite staircase of sound. Headphones or good speakers recommended.
